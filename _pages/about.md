I am an M.S. student in Statistical Mathematics at the University of Puerto Rico, Mayagüez (UPRM), where I also earned my B.A. in Economics. My research sits at the intersection of economics and statistics: **Bayesian inference, spatial econometrics, causal inference, and time series analysis**, applied mostly to Puerto Rico's labor market, productivity, and food security.

I also build the data infrastructure behind that research. As Lead Developer for the Puerto Rico Planning Board and for UPRM's Agricultural Experiment Station (SEA), I led teams that built data pipelines, APIs, and web platforms for economic indicators, food security, and agricultural surveys. Many of these tools are open source under the [gitinference](https://github.com/gitinference) organization.

Research interests
======
* **Labor economics:** the effect of minimum wage changes (and the Kaitz Index) on employment across sectors and between foreign and domestic businesses.
* **Productivity and development:** hierarchical Bayesian production models of total factor productivity across Puerto Rico's 78 municipalities, and the Human Development Index for Puerto Rico.
* **Health and food security:** the causal effect of living in a food desert on chronic disease mortality.
* **Spatial methods:** models that control for spatial variability, the subject of my thesis research.
